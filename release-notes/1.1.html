Convert to AVIF, HEIC, JPEG or PNG

  • A new Convert menu in the toolbar (also in Settings › Convert and the Optimize menu) re-encodes every dropped image in the format you pick before optimizing. AVIF gives the smallest files; the encoder is the one built into macOS, so nothing else is installed.
  • With a format chosen, HEIC, WebP, TIFF and BMP files can be dropped too, and folder drops pick them up.
  • The converted file takes the original's name with the new extension; the original goes to the Trash so Revert to Original still works, or keep it with a switch in Settings.
  • Converted JPEGs and PNGs are then run through the usual optimizers. Animated GIFs and SVGs stay in their own format.
