Imager shrinks images with these open-source tools, built from their sources and bundled inside the app:

Zopfli by Google (Apache 2.0) · OptiPNG by Cosmin Truta (zlib) · pngcrush by Glenn Randers-Pehrson (pngcrush license) · AdvanceCOMP by Andrea Mazzoleni (GPL 3) · pngquant and libimagequant by Kornel Lesiński (GPL 3) · jpegoptim by Timo Kokkonen (GPL 2) · MozJPEG by Mozilla and the libjpeg-turbo contributors (IJG, BSD and zlib) · Gifsicle by Eddie Kohler (GPL 2) · SVGO by Kir Belevich and contributors (MIT) · Sparkle (MIT).

Modeled on ImageOptim by Kornel Lesiński, with thanks.
